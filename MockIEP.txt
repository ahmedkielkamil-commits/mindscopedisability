Academic Performance:
Marcus is currently performing below grade level in reading and written 
expression. On the Woodcock-Johnson IV administered in April 2025, Marcus 
scored at a 5th grade equivalent in reading fluency and a 4th grade 
equivalent in written expression. His math performance is at grade level

Marcus has difficulty with phonemic decoding, reading fluency, and 
organizing written work. He often struggles to complete reading-based 
assignments within standard time limits and has difficulty taking notes 
during lectures.

Functional Performance:
Marcus demonstrates strong verbal communication skills and participates

and has a strong interest in history and technology. Marcus can become 
frustrated when faced with extended reading tasks and occasionally 
disengages from assignments that require sustained written output.

Effect on General Curriculum:
Marcus's learning disability affects his ability to access grade-level 
reading materials and demonstrate knowledge through written assessments, 
impacting his progress across all subjects that rely on reading and writing.
